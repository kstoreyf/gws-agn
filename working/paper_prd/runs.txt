Runs the paper needs (draft of 16 Nov 2026; nothing here has been launched).
Each run fills the red and teal macros named in placeholders.txt. Reasons are in notes/GAPS.md.

Common to all runs
  Code: darksirens-core at one pinned commit, every population value and every
    catalog-evaluation option set explicitly (owner's rule of 2 Oct 2026).
  Data: the v3 mock family (working/data/README.md). A "catalog" is one simulated
    catalog pair with its 1000 detected events and its injection set.
  Priors: uniform. H0 [50, 100] (two-parameter runs) or [60, 76] (host-dependent
    runs); f_AGN [0, 1]; log10 n_gal [-4, -1]; log10 n_AGN [-6, -4]; mass-peak
    location [20, 50] Msun and offset [-10, 10]; spin mean and offset as in
    Analysis 13; widths as in Analysis 13.
  Output: posterior samples or a grid, and the evidence. Bayes factors between
    nested models by Savage-Dickey where a grid allows, otherwise from evidences.
  Numerics: posterior samples per event and injection count set by run R0; the
    variance of ln L recorded in every run.

R0  Monte Carlo accuracy (decides the settings of everything below)
    One catalog. All-AGN event set, H0 only, and the mixed set, (H0, f_AGN).
    Redraw the posterior samples 20 times at 2,000 / 8,000 / 32,000 per event;
    redraw the injection set 5 times. Report the spread of the posterior median
    and width over redraws against the posterior width.            ~10 GPU-h
    Fills: the accuracy clause of Sec. V and Appendix A; closes gaps A1, A2.

R1  Complete catalogs, two populations: (H0, f_AGN). 10 catalogs.   Sec. IV A, V
R2  Complete catalogs, all events, galaxy catalog alone and AGN catalog alone:
    H0. 10 catalogs.                                                 Sec. IV B
R3  Event sets with all hosts galaxies and with all hosts AGN, matching catalog:
    H0. 20 catalogs each.                                            Sec. IV B
R4  Two-population model on event sets with no AGN-hosted mergers, and on a
    mixed set with input f_AGN = 0.10: (H0, f_AGN). 10 catalogs each. Sec. V
R5  Flux-limited catalogs (limits 21, 20, 19, 18), number densities at their
    inputs: (H0, f_AGN). 10 catalogs.                                Sec. IV C
R5b Same as R5 and R6 with the host population normalised over the catalog
    depth (survey z_depth at the catalog edge, or z_max at the edge) instead of
    0 < z < 5, and "complete" represented without a nominal magnitude limit.
    Tests the identity of claim L2: the response of f_AGN to the assumed AGN
    density should then scale with the uncatalogued share. 3 catalogs. Sec. IV C
R6  Same, AGN number density set to 0.5, 0.7, 1, 1.4, 2 times its input:
    (H0, f_AGN). 3 catalogs.                                         Sec. IV C
R7  Flux-limited catalogs, number densities free:
    (H0, f_AGN, log10 n_gal, log10 n_AGN), nested sampling. 5 catalogs, four
    limits.                                                          Sec. IV D
R8  Host-dependent mock (AGN mass peak +5 Msun, spin mean +0.10), two catalogs,
    nested sampling, 10 catalogs:
      a  common population: (H0, f_AGN)
      b  offsets free: + (mass-peak offset, spin-mean offset)
      c  reference population free: + (mass-peak location, spin mean)
      d  widths free: + (mass-peak width, spin width)
      e  common population with its own free parameters and no offsets:
         (H0, f_AGN, mass-peak location and width, spin mean and width).
         The fair single-population analysis; 8a fixes the population at
         the galaxy-class values.                                Sec. IV B, V
R9  Host-dependent mock, galaxy catalog alone, offset modelled and not modelled:
    3 catalogs.                                                      Sec. IV E
R10 Complete catalogs at twice the pixel resolution: (H0, f_AGN). 2 catalogs.
