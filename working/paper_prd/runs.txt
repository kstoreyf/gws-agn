Runs the paper needs (draft of 16 Nov 2026; nothing here has been launched).
Each run fills the red and teal macros named in placeholders.txt. Reasons are in notes/GAPS.md.

Common to all runs
  Code: darksirens-core at one pinned commit, every population value and every
    catalog-evaluation option set explicitly (owner's rule of 2 Oct 2026).
  Data: the v3 mock family (working/data/README.md). A "catalog" is one simulated
    catalog pair with its 1000 detected events and its injection set.
  Priors: uniform. H0 [50, 100] (two-parameter runs) or [60, 76] (host-dependent
    runs); f_AGN [0, 1]; log10 n_gal [-4, -1]; log10 n_AGN [-6, -4]; mass-peak
    location [20, 50] Msun and offset [-10, 10]; spin mean and offset as in
    Analysis 13; widths as in Analysis 13.
  Output: posterior samples or a grid, and the evidence. Bayes factors between
    nested models by Savage-Dickey where a grid allows, otherwise from evidences.
  Numerics: posterior samples per event and injection count set by run R0; the
    variance of ln L recorded in every run.

R0  Monte Carlo accuracy (decides the settings of everything below)
    One catalog. All-AGN event set, H0 only, and the mixed set, (H0, f_AGN).
    Redraw the posterior samples 20 times at 2,000 / 8,000 / 32,000 per event;
    redraw the injection set 5 times. Report the spread of the posterior median
    and width over redraws against the posterior width.            ~10 GPU-h
    Fills: the accuracy clause of Sec. V and Appendix A; closes gaps A1, A2.

REVISION OF 17 NOV (after REVIEW.md and REVISION_DECISIONS.md; supersedes the lines above where they differ)
  Production convention for every run below:
    - events regenerated with darksirens-core's population (working/data/corepop/);
    - hosts normalized over the redshift grid to z = 1.10; each class's number density from its catalog counts
      where the catalog is complete;
    - no population parameter that carries H0 is held at its input: the single-population model infers the
      mass-peak location and width and the spin mean and width with H0 and f_AGN (six parameters); the
      host-dependent model adds the two AGN offsets (eight);
    - H0 uniform on [50, 100] in every run; one prior range per parameter across models;
    - posterior samples per event and injections as Analysis 14 decides; variance of ln L recorded.
  R1'  complete catalogs, single population (6 parameters), equal-population mock.            Sec. IV A, V
  R8'  host-dependent mock: single population (6) against host-dependent populations (8).     Sec. IV B, V
       This is the lead comparison. The exploratory 8a to 8c become one sentence in the text.
  R11  no catalog (hosts uniform in comoving volume), single population, on the events of R1' and R8':
       H0 from the mass distribution alone.                                                   Sec. IV A, IV B
  R5', R6', R7'  flux-limited catalogs under the production convention: densities from the complete part;
       response to a wrong completeness model or density; densities free.                     Sec. IV C
  R2, R3, R4, R9, R10 as before, with the six-parameter single population in place of the fixed one.
  Option for the owner: a second mock family with a smaller fraction of mergers in the Gaussian peak.
  Realizations: about ten for R1' and R8', five for the others. The cost comes with the plan, after Analysis 14.
