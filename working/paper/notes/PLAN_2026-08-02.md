## Headline decision (gated on production numbers already on disk)

The joint two-tracer measurement: with 1000 dark sirens and two complete catalogs
tracing one density field, (H0, f_AGN) are measured JOINTLY and both recover —
H0 = 69.2 +1.0/−1.0 vs truth 67.74 and f = 0.273 +0.049/−0.047 vs realised 0.295
on the reference realisation; over five end-to-end realisations the mean offsets
are +0.41 ± 0.55 (H0) and −0.012 ± 0.020 (f). The joint H0 interval (1.94) is
1.7× SHARPER than the galaxy catalog alone (3.30) — adding the sparse tracer and
its mixture weight does not cost H0 precision, it buys it. Supporting contrast:
single-catalog analyses on the same mixed universe are mis-specified — AGN-only
rails at the top of the scanned range (quoted as such, entry null), GAL-only sits
at 69.9 +1.7/−1.6. Null: sky-shuffling the events collapses f to 0.037
[0.004, 0.106] vs record 0.266 — the f weight is host association.
ρ(H0, f) = +0.105 ± 0.035 (weak degeneracy).

Assumption costs to state plainly: fixed source population (informative, not
free); Om0 pinned; both catalogs complete (incompleteness = analysis 3, pending);
n0 and bias of each tracer known exactly; photo-z model known; f closure judged
against the realised per-seed host fraction (binomial gap to planted 0.30
documented); mock's own detection/PE family (v3, literature-anchored).
